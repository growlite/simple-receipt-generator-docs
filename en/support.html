A document looks wrong

Check seller/customer information, items, currency, tax treatment and dates before issuing. Drafts can be edited; issued content is locked. Use the available correction, replacement, void or refund workflow when appropriate, reviewing the result and its preserved history. Do not record a refund to hide a mistaken entry: a recorded refund should describe money actually returned. Contact support using a synthetic example if the available action does not fit your situation. The app does not provide tax or legal advice.

A PDF will not save or share

Try generating the PDF again and check available device storage and the selected Files or Share destination. Open the resulting file to verify the content and location. A destination may need its own account or connection. Cancelling delivery may still leave a generated PDF in app storage. A PDF is a readable copy, not a full backup; sharing it does not remove the original or any earlier copies.

iCloud sync is pending or shows a conflict

Open the app's iCloud sync settings and read the status. Check that the intended iCloud account and connectivity are available, then use Sync now if enabled. An unavailable or pending status does not prove another device received the records. Compare the document and its linked history on the other device before relying on convergence.

If the app reports an account change, review the account confirmation carefully: local documents can be uploaded to the new account and copies remain in the old one. For a conflict, review the complete linked group before choosing a version. Both versions remain preserved; selecting a version does not erase the other. Issued-content conflicts may require a later correction or replacement rather than a rewrite.

Do not remove the app, erase iCloud storage or repeatedly change accounts as a first troubleshooting step. These actions can affect records and are not a verified repair or complete deletion method. Turning sync off retains copies and is not a backup.
