Data summary

Version: 1.0 · Effective: 27 September 2026

What you put in the app

Simple Receipt Generator uses business settings, Inventory items and receipt/invoice details to create and manage documents. Documents may contain seller/customer names, contact and tax details, items, dates, amounts and payment/refund references. Enter only information you are entitled to use. Do not enter card numbers, bank passwords or unnecessary sensitive data. Choosing a payment method records a fact; it does not process a payment.

Where copies can go

  • On this device: records, autosaved drafts, settings, Inventory, search indexes, sync metadata, conflict versions and generated PDFs.
  • Optional private iCloud: saved documents and linked payment, correction and refund history, including details embedded in those documents. Sync starts off. Global Inventory/settings and PDF files do not independently sync; this is not full backup.
  • Files or Share: a PDF copy goes to the destination you choose. That recipient or service controls its copy. A PDF is not a complete restore archive.
  • Other channels: operating-system backups may hold app data. Support email and TestFlight feedback can contain information you send. Apple services can provide diagnostics and usage information separately from document sync.

What the controls actually do

Editing defaults does not rewrite previous documents. Eligible drafts can be deleted; deletion removes the active record but can leave sync identifiers and storage remnants. Issued receipts/invoices and their correction, void and refund history remain. Conflict versions remain even after resolution. The current app has no Inventory deletion, whole-profile deletion, conflict-archive purge or generated-PDF cleanup control.

Switching off iCloud sync keeps local and existing cloud copies; a submitted operation can finish. Changing accounts does not remove former cloud copies. Exported PDFs, backups and copies on other devices are separate. No action here is a promise to erase every copy or securely overwrite storage.
