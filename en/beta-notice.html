What is and is not established

The app records receipt, invoice, payment and refund facts and exports PDFs; it does not move money. iCloud sync is optional document sync, not full backup. Turning it off retains copies. Issued history and conflict versions remain, and exported copies cannot be recalled.

At preparation of this notice, real physical-device offline/rejoin, a same-account second endpoint in the production cloud environment, iCloud Files delivery, real VoiceOver and minimum-iOS validation remain release gates. A Simulator or a successful local test does not prove the distributed build works on your device. The exact signed archive, processed TestFlight build and production-cloud behavior must be verified separately. No completion of those gates is claimed here.

Data shared through TestFlight

Apple automatically collects beta crash and usage information and shares it with the app provider. It can include invitation identity and device/build information. Optional feedback adds your comments or screenshots and related diagnostic context. Disabling the app's document sync does not stop this beta-service sharing. Avoid real customer data in test records, screenshots and comments.

Apple's TestFlight privacy notice explains its own handling, including its retention of beta feedback for one year and potentially longer retention of crash/usage information until bugs are resolved. Those are Apple's periods; the separate GrowLite policy below applies only to data under its control. See Apple's TestFlight privacy notice.

When Apple makes feedback, reports, analytics or logs available to GrowLite, we use them to investigate problems and improve the app.

GrowLite AI LLC's policy is not to export Apple/TestFlight diagnostic reports, analytics or logs outside Apple, to retain diagnostic/feedback data it controls for one year and to delete that data when asked at support@growlite.ai. This does not promise deletion of Apple's own service data or logs. We handle retention manually, use available feedback-deletion controls and stop using identifiable diagnostics covered by a deletion request. These actions do not erase Apple-controlled logs.

Reporting a problem and leaving the test

Send steps, expected and actual results, version/build, device and iOS version through available TestFlight feedback or support@growlite.ai. Use invented examples and redact screenshots. Never send account passwords or verification codes. If behavior threatens your records, stop using that workflow and report it; do not erase app or cloud data as an improvised repair.

You can stop testing and use TestFlight's available participation controls. Stopping, removing a beta or turning sync off does not guarantee deletion of Apple feedback, Provider-held messages, cloud records, retained exports or backups. Use support@growlite.ai for privacy requests about Provider-held information and Apple's privacy controls for Apple-held information. No remote wipe, complete recovery or full erasure is promised.
