2. Information used to run the app

The app stores your business name and selected region, time zone, currency, language, appearance and tax defaults. It stores reusable Inventory items, including names, descriptions, prices and currencies, and remembers dismissed item suggestions. A Home preference controls whether pending invoices are included.

Documents can contain seller name, address and tax ID; customer name, email, phone, address and tax ID; item names, descriptions, quantities and prices; dates, currency, tax choices and amounts. Payment records contain method, date and reference. Issued IDs, invoice/receipt links, correction reasons, replacement history, void dates and refund facts support the document history. The app records these facts; it does not process payments or request payment-card credentials. Free text can nevertheless contain sensitive information, so enter only what is needed.

The app also keeps record identifiers, timestamps, search summaries and indexes, sync status and account binding, change-comparison metadata, deletion markers and complete conflicting document versions. These support finding records, displaying totals and preventing accidental duplication or overwrite. Search indexes derive from document content; the app does not keep a separate history of your search queries. Draft autosave can store information before you issue a document.

System language and time zone help initialize preferences. Manually entered addresses and regions are not GPS readings. The app does not request access to Contacts, camera, microphone, photos, location or health data. It has no app login, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, third-party payment SDK or developer-operated document server in this version. This does not mean Apple services, support or a future website process no data.

3. Local storage and optional iCloud document sync

Records, profile, Inventory, preferences, search projections, sync metadata and generated PDFs are stored in the app's device storage. The app uses them to provide the features described above. Device backups or device-management features may handle copies separately from the app's sync switch.

Document sync is optional and initially off. If you enable it, saved receipts and invoices, including their embedded seller/customer/item/default details and linked payment, correction and refund history, are sent to an Apple-operated private CloudKit database associated with the current iCloud account. Record identifiers, change metadata and account binding support synchronization. Apple operates that service under its own terms and privacy practices. The app can access the private documents on devices using that account. This architecture is not a public document-sharing database and does not establish that the Provider can retrieve users' private document content through a developer console.

Sync runs in the foreground or when you use Sync now, subject to service availability. Global Inventory, business defaults, appearance and PDF files do not independently sync; their values copied into documents are part of those documents. Sync is not a complete backup. Turning it off stops new sync work but retains local data and existing cloud copies, and an operation already sent may finish. When the iCloud account changes, the app pauses for confirmation; using the new account can send local documents to it while former cloud copies remain.

4. PDFs, recipients and external links

Generating a PDF uses document information locally. Save to Files and Share deliver copies to the destination you select, which may be another app, a person or a cloud storage provider. That destination handles its copy under its own terms. Review the recipient and the PDF before sending. The app cannot recall a delivered copy.

Successful generated PDFs remain in app storage, including after cancellation of delivery or repeated export. Export staging and sync upload files can temporarily contain full document information; cleanup is attempted but can be interrupted or fail. Apple controls its download-cache lifecycle. A PDF contains a rendered subset of the record and is not a complete machine-readable data export or restore file.

Opening an official tax-source link launches an external website. The app does not append your document data to that link. The browser and site may process network metadata and cookies under their own policies. The bundled tax suggestions are not a live tax-data feed.

6. Retention, deletion and your available controls

The app has no automatic age-based purge. You can edit drafts and delete eligible drafts. Draft deletion removes the active logical record and its search summary, but standalone deletion leaves an identifier marker to support sync. Replacement drafts follow their linked original's history. Database storage remnants are not covered by a secure-erasure guarantee.

Issued receipts and invoices cannot be generically deleted or rewritten; voids, corrections and refunds preserve history. Editing business defaults or Inventory does not change old document snapshots. There is no whole-profile delete, Inventory deletion/dismissed-suggestion reset, conflict-archive purge or generated-PDF cleanup control in the current app. Conflict versions remain after resolution. Turning sync off does not delete them. Logical removal during sync is not complete cloud-container erasure, and server historical retention has not been verified.

Copies may also remain on other devices, with PDF recipients, in Files providers and in device backups. Removing the app from a device is not a verified method to erase those copies; offloading can preserve local data. There is no universal delete-everywhere or complete-data-download function. Do not rely on sync-off, draft deletion or a PDF export as proof of complete erasure or backup.
